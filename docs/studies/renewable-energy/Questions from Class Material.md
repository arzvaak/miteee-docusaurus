# Questions from Class Material

These are literal questions taken from lecture slides, assignments, and class handouts. They test recall and comprehension of specific topics as presented in class.

## CO1 — Energy Basics and Renewable Overview

1. List out the various primary and secondary energy sources. Explain those energy sources with special reference to Indian context.
2. What are primary and secondary energy sources and conventional and non-conventional energy sources? Describe in brief.
3. Discuss briefly the driving forces of world energy supply and demand.
4. Classify the energy resources. Discuss the role of renewable energy sources in combating global warming and promoting economic development of a country.
5. List out the advantages, prospects and different forms of renewable energy sources.
6. List out the different forms of renewable energy sources and explain any five of them.
7. What is renewable energy? Mention its advantages and disadvantages.
8. Explain various advantages and limitations of the implementation of renewable energy sources.
9. Differentiate between energy conservation and energy efficiency. Explain their importance in the present energy scenario.
10. Mention the advantages and limitations of renewable energy sources.
11. With a neat sketch explain an MHD open cycle system.
12. With schematic diagram explain the working of an acidic H₂-O₂ fuel cell.
13. Calculate thermionic generator efficiency given: $phi_c = 3$ V, $phi_a = 2$ V, $T_c = 2300$ K, $T_a = 1000$ K, $A_c = 1$ m², $epsilon = 0.3$, $A = 1.2 times 10^6$ A/m²K².
14. List the various renewable energy policy initiatives in India.

## CO2 — Solar Energy

15. Determine the local solar time and declination at latitude 23°15' N, longitude 77°30' E at 12:30 IST on June 19. EoT = −1'01".
16. Determine the average daily global radiation on a horizontal surface for June 22 at latitude 10° N. Given: $a = 0.30$, $b = 0.51$, $overline(n)/N = 0.55$.
17. Explain the construction and working of a flat-plate collector with a neat sketch.
18. Derive the expression for angle of incidence of solar radiation on a tilted south-facing collector.
19. Derive expression for conversion efficiency and power output of a solar cell.
20. Describe effect of insolation and temperature on PV cell performance with graphs.
21. Draw and explain the equivalent circuit of a practical PV cell. Draw the I-V curve and comment on fill factor.
22. Derive I-V characteristics for a practical PV cell (single diode model). Draw I-V/P-V for different conditions. Calculate $V_"MPP"$ and $FF$.
23. A heterojunction solar cell (Area 6 cm²) has $V_"OC" = 400$ mV, $I_"SC" = 200$ mA under 0.8 sun. $FF = 80%$. Find efficiency.
24. Explain the importance of MPPT and derive the buck-boost converter voltage relationship.
25. Design a PV system with battery for a given load.
26. Explain mismatch losses in solar PV modules, their sources, and protection methods.
27. Describe problems faced by solar PV cells connected in series under partial shading.

## CO3 — Wind Energy

28. Calculate the wind power density at a site with average wind speed 15 m/s at 50 m height, average temperature 17°C, and elevation 900 m above sea level.
29. Prove that the maximum power extracted from wind is 59.3% (Betz limit) with a velocity/pressure diagram.
30. Calculate power density, power, torque, and thrust for given wind/turbine data.
31. Explain with sketches the working of HAWT and VAWT wind turbines.
32. Classify wind turbines and explain the DFIG wind turbine system.
33. Explain the basic components of a WECS with a block diagram.
34. Compare Savonius and Darrieus type wind turbines.
35. Design a standalone PV-wind-battery system using capacity factors.
36. Estimate wind turbine power rating using capacity factor for a given energy requirement.
37. Explain the working of a solar-wind hybrid system with battery storage.
38. Explain the advantages of hybrid systems and the control strategy of a wind-battery hybrid.

## CO4 — Biomass Energy

39. Define energy plantation. List its advantages, disadvantages, and common plant species.
40. Explain the different biomass conversion technologies with a neat diagram.
41. Describe the principle of anaerobic digestion and the effect of pH, C/N ratio, and temperature on biogas generation.
42. Design a family biogas digester for 5 cows. Given: retention time 20 days, dry matter 2 kg/cow/day, biogas yield 0.24 m³/kg, burner efficiency 60%, methane proportion 0.8, methane CV 28 MJ/m³.
43. Compare and discuss the working principles of updraft and downdraft gasifiers.
44. Explain the construction and working of a downdraft gasifier with a diagram.
45. Explain the operation of bubbling fluidized-bed and circulating fluidized-bed gasifiers with diagrams.
46. Calculate engine power from gasifier efficiency and biomass feed rate: biomass feed rate 320 kg/h, gasifier efficiency 0.65, biomass CV 16,800 kJ/kg, engine efficiency 45%.
47. Explain the working, benefits, and limitations of a landfill reactor for MSW disposal.

## CO5 — Geothermal and Ocean Energy

48. Explain dry steam, flash steam, and binary cycle geothermal power plants with neat diagrams.
49. Compare flash steam and binary cycle systems. List environmental problems.
50. Explain power generation through Hot Dry Rock (HDR) technology.
51. Explain the closed-cycle (Anderson) OTEC system with a schematic diagram.
52. Describe the open-cycle (Claude) OTEC system with a T-S diagram.
53. Explain the hybrid OTEC cycle and how it overcomes the drawbacks of closed and open cycles.
54. Compare double basin linked basin and paired basin tidal plants.
55. Calculate tidal power for: tidal range 10 m, basin area 0.55 km², average head 9.5 m, efficiency 75%.
56. Explain the Dolphin type wave power machine with a diagram.
57. Derive the expression for energy/power in a single basin tidal system.
58. Explain the float wave-power conversion device with a diagram.
