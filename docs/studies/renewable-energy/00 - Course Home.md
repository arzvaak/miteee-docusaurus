## Course Overview

This course covers the fundamentals of renewable energy systems, from energy classification and advanced conversion technologies through the detailed design and analysis of solar, wind, biomass, geothermal, and ocean energy systems. The material is organized into 18 chapters following the five Course Outcomes (COs) of the ELE 4086 syllabus.

> **Exam Boundary:** The formal examination for ELE 4086 covers **Chapters 1–11** (CO1 through CO3: Energy Fundamentals, Solar PV, and Wind Energy). Chapters 12–18 (CO4 and CO5: Biomass, Geothermal, and Ocean Energy) are included for completeness and reference.

## Recommended Study Path

1. **Energy Fundamentals (Ch 1–3):** Start with energy classification, then advanced conversion technologies (FBC, MHD, thermionic, fuel cells), and conclude with an overview of renewable energy characteristics and applications.

2. **Solar Energy (Ch 4–7):** Begin with solar radiation physics and geometry (Ch 4), then solar thermal collectors (Ch 5), photovoltaic cell physics (Ch 6), and finish with PV system design and MPPT (Ch 7).

3. **Wind Energy (Ch 8–11):** Study wind resource assessment and site selection (Ch 8), then aerodynamics and the Betz limit (Ch 9), followed by WECS classification and generator technologies (Ch 10), and hybrid wind systems with storage (Ch 11).

4. **Biomass Energy (Ch 12–15):** Explore biomass resources and conversion pathways (Ch 12), biogas production and digester design (Ch 13), gasification technologies (Ch 14), and waste-to-energy systems (Ch 15).

5. **Geothermal and Ocean Energy (Ch 16–18):** Study geothermal power plants (Ch 16), OTEC systems (Ch 17), and wave and tidal energy conversion (Ch 18).
