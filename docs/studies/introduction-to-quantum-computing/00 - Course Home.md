---

## Course Overview

This course introduces the principles of quantum computing, beginning with the mathematical machinery of complex vector spaces and progressing through quantum states, gates, circuits, entanglement, and foundational quantum algorithms. The material is grounded in a 135-page Unit IV lecture deck and supplemented by syllabus-matched exam papers.

**Reference Textbooks:**
1. *Quantum Computation and Quantum Information* — Nielsen & Chuang (Cambridge, 10th Anniversary Ed., 2010)
2. *An Introduction to Quantum Computing* — Kaye, Laflamme & Mosca (Oxford, 1st Ed., 2007)
3. *Quantum Computing: A Beginner's Introduction* — Parag K. Lala (McGraw-Hill, 1st Ed., 2019)
4. *Quantum Mechanics: Concepts and Applications* — Nouredine Zettili (Wiley India, 2nd Ed., 2016)

---

## Immediate Exam Boundary

The deck scope is **Unit IV — Introduction to Quantum Computing (12 hours)**. The following topics are confirmed within the deck:

- Complex vector spaces and Dirac notation
- Linear dependence/independence, spanning sets, bases, dimension
- Inner products and norms
- Operators, matrix representations
- Eigenvalues and eigenvectors
- Hermitian, unitary, and normal operators
- Quantum postulates and measurement
- Qubits and the Bloch sphere
- Quantum gates (Pauli, Hadamard, CNOT, Toffoli, Fredkin, SWAP, Phase, T)
- Quantum circuits
- Multi-qubit systems and tensor products
- Entanglement and Bell states
- Quantum algorithms present in the deck exercises (Shor's code/algorithm, Deutsch-Jozsa, Grover's search, quantum teleportation, BB84, superdense coding, QFT)
