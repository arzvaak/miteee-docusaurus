## Best use before the exam

1. For each topic, explain the recognition cue and formula conditions before calculating.
2. Rework all original numerical problems without looking at the solution.
3. In Switch, choose a week and target weak methods; bookmarks keep difficult questions together.
4. Finish with exact-assignment practice for both types, then the quick revision sheets.
5. Read [Source Cautions](/notes/studies-power-system-protection-and-switchgear-exam-preparation-source-cautions) so that an ambiguous key does not become a misconception.

Your older personal notes remain untouched. The new notes live only in this Exam Preparation folder.
