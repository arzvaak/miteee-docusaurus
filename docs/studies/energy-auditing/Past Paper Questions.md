## ELE 423 Papers

### PYQ 01 — 2014 Regular (ELE 423)

*MIT Manipal, VII Sem B.E. (E&E), End Semester Examination, 09 December 2013. Time 3 hrs; Max Marks 50. "Answer any FIVE full questions."*

**Q1.** (a) Explain the three classifications of energy, with examples. (b) With a neat flowchart, explain the steps of Energy Action Planning. (c) With respect to the EC Act 2001, define (i) Building, (ii) Energy Audit. List any four duties of a designated consumer.

**Q2.** (a) Explain in detail the ten steps of Detailed Energy Audit methodology. (b) With respect to the Electricity Act 2003, explain the reforms in generation, transmission, and distribution; tariff principles; and the role of CEA.

**Q3.** (a) Laws of conservation: Skim milk problem — 90.5% water, 3.5% protein, 5.1% carbohydrate, 0.1% fat, 0.8% ash. Original milk had 4.5% fat. Calculate original composition. (b) How does Force Field Analysis help in energy policy & planning? (c) List four duties and four responsibilities of an Energy Manager.

**Q4.** (a) Plant runs 8,000 h/yr, 80%-efficient boiler, 15 kg/cm² steam. Pipeline: 100 mm dia, 300 m total, 150 m uninsulated at 195 °C. Proposal: insulate with 70 mm glass wool (surface drops to 70 °C). Fuel Rs. 15/kg, GCV 14,250 kcal/kg, insulation Rs. 4,000/m². Find fuel savings, cost savings, and payback. (b) Elaborate on India's Integrated Energy Policy: energy security, efficiency/conservation, power sector reforms.

**Q5.** (a) Explain the 5S management tool with significance of each S, implementation technique, and benefits. (b) What is PAT scheme? Explain with an example. (c) List four energy conservation measures for a building.

### PYQ 07 — 2018 Regular (ELE 4006)

**Q1.** (a) Fill in the blanks: Commercial energy is ___. UDAY is ___. Strategic oil reserve in Rajasthan is ___. Act to transform power sector is ___. NAPCC has ___ missions. (b) A spherical balloon (diameter 5 m) is filled with natural gas. How much energy is contained?

**Q2.** (a) Write a note on technical and economic feasibility of energy conservation opportunities. (b) Explain energy audit methodology for a textile industry.

**Q3.** (a) Explain the concept of baseline and target setting in energy management. (b) A plant has monthly data. Regression: $E = 0.3P + 150$. Calculate CUSUM for 6 months and savings.

**Q4.** (a) Explain different types of tariffs with examples. (b) A consumer has MD = 250 kW, consumption = 120,000 kWh, tariff = Rs. 60/kW + Rs. 5/kWh. Calculate bill.

**Q5.** (a) Explain power factor improvement and its benefits. (b) A 200 kW load at 0.7 PF lag. Improve to 0.95 lag. Find kVAR and annual savings.

**Q6.** (a) What is DSM? Explain with examples. (b) Explain LED lighting program in India.

**Q7.** (a) Explain boiler efficiency calculation — direct and indirect methods. (b) A boiler produces 8 tonnes/hr steam at 15 bar, 0.97 dry. Feed water 90 °C. Coal consumption 1,200 kg/hr, GCV 5,500 kcal/kg. Calculate efficiency.

**Q8.** (a) Explain flash steam recovery. (b) Condensate at 12 kg/cm²g flashes to 2 kg/cm²g. Flow 1,500 kg/hr. Find flash steam quantity.

**Q9.** (a) What is a heat exchanger? Explain LMTD. (b) Counterflow heat exchanger: hot fluid 150 °C to 90 °C; cold fluid 40 °C to 80 °C. Calculate LMTD.

**Q10.** (a) What is cogeneration? Explain advantages. (b) Compare CHP efficiency with separate generation.
