## Topic Notes

### What is Political Theory?

Political theory is a systematic reflection on politics, dealing with ideas and concepts that shape political life. It asks fundamental questions about the state, government, power, rights, justice, freedom, equality, and democracy. It is a normative discipline that evaluates political practices and prescribes ideals.

### Politics and Political Theory

Politics involves the activities of cooperation, conflict, and negotiation in collective decision-making. Political theory provides the conceptual tools to understand and critically assess these activities. It goes beyond description to analyze what is just, fair, and desirable.

### Difference between Political Theory and Political Science

Political science is primarily empirical, studying political institutions and behavior through observation and data. Political theory is normative, focusing on values, principles, and ideals. It asks 'what should be' rather than 'what is'.

### Key Concepts in Political Theory

Major concepts include freedom (liberty), equality, justice, democracy, authority, legitimacy, sovereignty, rights, and duties. These concepts are interpreted differently across ideologies, leading to debates on their meaning and application.

### The Evolution of Political Theory

Political theory has evolved from ancient Greek philosophy (Plato, Aristotle) to medieval Christian thought (Augustine, Aquinas), modern social contract theories (Hobbes, Locke, Rousseau), and contemporary discussions on liberalism, socialism, feminism, and post-colonialism. Recent trends include focus on multiculturalism, environmentalism, and global justice.

### Significance of Political Theory

Political theory helps us understand political events, critically evaluate government policies, and form reasoned opinions about public affairs. It provides a framework for analyzing issues like inequality, discrimination, and oppression, and guides political action and reform.

### Political Theory and Ideology

An ideology is a set of beliefs that reflects the interests of a particular group. Political theory, while sometimes overlapping with ideology, seeks a more rational and universal basis for its claims. It subjects ideologies to critical scrutiny, examining their internal consistency and practical implications.

- Meaning of satyagraha: Soul force, civil disobedience, non-violence (ahimsa) as cardinal principle.
