# Power System Protection and Switchgear

This subject is my complete eight-week study route for power-system protection and switchgear. It follows the NPTEL course taught by Prof. Bhaveshkumar R. Bhalja at IIT Roorkee, with the lecture PDF and assignment screenshots processed using Mistral OCR 4 and the weekly notes drafted with DeepSeek V4 Flash against that extracted evidence.

## What I will learn

By the end of the course, I should be able to:

- explain how protection zones, relays, instrument transformers, and circuit breakers work together;
- select and coordinate overcurrent, directional, differential, distance, and carrier-aided schemes;
- protect transmission lines, transformers, generators, induction motors, and busbars;
- analyze auto-reclosing, synchronizing, transients, surges, and arc interruption;
- compare circuit-breaker technologies and understand relay testing, commissioning, and maintenance;
- solve numerical, analytical, and troubleshooting questions using protection-engineering checks.

## How I should study each week

1. Read the learning outcomes and syllabus map before the full theory.
2. Trace each operating sequence through the Mermaid diagrams and source visuals.
3. Rework the examples without looking at the solution.
4. Use the comparison tables to separate similar relay and breaker characteristics.
5. Attempt all 18 questions before opening their answer blocks.
6. Finish with the quick-revision sheet and revisit any protection-engineering check I missed.
