# Water hammer

The accumulated condensate forms a slug \(a large plug of water\)\.

High\-velocity steam behind the slug pushes it forward at high speed — sometimes over 100 km/h\.

When this high\-mass slug of condensate hits a bend\, valve\, or fitting\, it causes a sudden impact called water hammer\.

This produces loud knocking sounds and can damage pipes\, valves\, and equipment due to the enormous pressure shock\.

❌  __Condition:__  Dangerous \(steam hammer risk\)

💥  __Effect:__  Pipe vibration\, joint failure\, or mechanical damage
