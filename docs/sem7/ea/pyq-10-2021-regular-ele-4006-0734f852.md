## Worked numerical solution — furnace fuel requirement

**Worked solution - not an official answer**

Useful heat per tonne of charge:

$$1{,}000\times0.12\times(1{,}150-50)=132,000\ \text{kcal/tonne}$$

At 32% furnace efficiency, fuel CV $10{,}000$ kcal/kg and density $0.95$ kg/L:

$$\frac{132{,}000}{0.32\times10{,}000\times0.95}=43.42\ \text{L/tonne}$$

For 15 tonne/hr:

$$43.42\times15=651.3\ \text{L/hr}$$

**Final answer:** useful heat = **132,000 kcal/tonne**; specific fuel consumption = **43.42 L/tonne**; total fuel consumption = **651.3 L/hr**. Unit check: L/tonne multiplied by tonne/hr gives L/hr.
