## 1. Match Trophies to the Correct Sport

| Trophy or cup | Sport and level | Trap |
|---|---|---|
| Ranji Trophy | Indian first-class cricket | not football |
| Duleep Trophy | Indian first-class cricket | not badminton |
| Vijay Hazare Trophy | Indian one-day cricket | not Test cricket |
| Santosh Trophy | Indian football | not hockey |
| Durand Cup | football | not cricket |
| Thomas Cup | men’s team badminton | Uber Cup is women’s team event |
| Sudirman Cup | mixed-team badminton | not singles-only |
| Davis Cup | men’s team tennis | not badminton |
| Sultan Azlan Shah Cup | field hockey | not football |

Tournament sponsors and official titles can change, so focus on the underlying competition and sport. If a question asks the **current holder**, use the edition year and a dated source.

**Worked example**

Prompt: “Thomas Cup is associated with which sport?” The team-event pair is **badminton**. Davis Cup is the nearby tennis trap.

**Self-check**

Which Indian domestic trophy is associated with football: Santosh Trophy or Ranji Trophy?

<details>
<summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

Santosh Trophy. Ranji Trophy is a first-class cricket competition.
</details>

## 2. Use Terms and Team Sizes as Classifiers

| Sport | Players on field/court per side | Signature terms |
|---|---:|---|
| Football | 11 | offside, penalty kick |
| Cricket | 11 | wicket, over, follow-on |
| Field hockey | 11 | penalty corner, bully |
| Basketball | 5 | dribble, rebound, slam dunk |
| Volleyball | 6 | serve, set, spike, libero |
| Kabaddi | 7 | raid, bonus line, all-out |
| Baseball | 9 | inning, pitcher, home run |

Tennis uses love, deuce, ace, and tie-break. Golf uses par, birdie, eagle, and bogey. Chess uses check, checkmate, stalemate, and castling. A “hat-trick” can occur in several sports, so it is not an exclusive football term.

**Worked example**

Prompt: “Birdie and eagle are used in which sport?” Both compare a hole score with par, so the answer is **golf**.

**Self-check**

How many players from one volleyball team are on court in standard play?

<details>
<summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

Six. A basketball team has five on court; football, cricket, and field hockey use eleven per side.
</details>

## 3. Understand Major Multi-Sport Events

The Summer and Winter Olympic Games are separate events, each ordinarily held on a four-year cycle. The Asian Games and Commonwealth Games also normally follow four-year cycles. The Paralympic Games are elite multi-sport events for athletes with disabilities; the Special Olympics serve athletes with intellectual disabilities. They are distinct movements.

The five Olympic rings are a symbol of the Olympic Movement and the meeting of athletes worldwide. Do not memorise a colour-to-continent mapping: the International Olympic Committee does not assign each ring colour to one specific continent.

Hosts, mascots, slogans, medal tables, newly added disciplines, and qualification rules are edition-specific. Preserve the year with every such fact.

**Self-check**

Can the colour of one Olympic ring be safely matched to one named continent?

<details>
<summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

No. That popular mapping is not an official IOC assignment.
</details>

## 4. Separate Civilian, Gallantry, and Sports Honours

India’s civilian honours follow the order **Bharat Ratna**, **Padma Vibhushan**, **Padma Bhushan**, and **Padma Shri**. Bharat Ratna is the highest civilian award; the Padma awards are not military gallantry decorations.

The **Param Vir Chakra** is India’s highest wartime gallantry award. The **Ashoka Chakra** is the highest peacetime gallantry award. Maha Vir Chakra and Vir Chakra belong to the wartime series; Kirti Chakra and Shaurya Chakra to the peacetime series.

National Sports Award names, categories, eligibility rules, and recipients are governed by current official schemes. The Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna has been the top national sporting honour under its present name, the Arjuna Award recognises sporting achievement, and the Dronacharya Award recognises coaches—but verify any latest scheme or recipient in the question year.

**Worked example**

Prompt: “Which is the highest wartime gallantry award?” The word **wartime** selects **Param Vir Chakra**, not Ashoka Chakra.

**Self-check**

Which ranks higher in the civilian order: Padma Vibhushan or Padma Bhushan?

<details>
<summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

Padma Vibhushan.
</details>

## 5. Learn Historic Firsts Without Turning Them into Records

Historic firsts remain fixed; “most,” “latest,” and “current record” can change.

- **Rabindranath Tagore** became the first Asian Nobel laureate in 1913, receiving the Literature prize.
- **C. V. Raman** received the 1930 Nobel Prize in Physics for work connected with the Raman effect.
- **Abhinav Bindra** won India’s first individual Olympic gold medal, in the 10 m air rifle at Beijing 2008.
- **Karnam Malleswari** became the first Indian woman to win an Olympic medal, at Sydney 2000.
- **Neeraj Chopra** won India’s first Olympic gold medal in athletics, in the men’s javelin at Tokyo 2020 (held in 2021).
- **Rakesh Sharma** became the first Indian citizen to travel in space, in 1984.

Keep event name and year with the first. Do not infer that the person still holds a current record.

**Self-check**

Who won India’s first individual Olympic gold medal?

<details>
<summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

Abhinav Bindra, in the 10 m air rifle at the Beijing 2008 Olympic Games.
</details>

## 6. Pair Books with Authors Precisely

| Book | Author association | Precision note |
|---|---|---|
| *The Discovery of India* | Jawaharlal Nehru | historical-cultural work |
| *Wings of Fire* | A. P. J. Abdul Kalam with Arun Tiwari | autobiography; do not drop co-author |
| *Playing It My Way* | Sachin Tendulkar with Boria Majumdar | autobiography |
| *The God of Small Things* | Arundhati Roy | novel |
| *Train to Pakistan* | Khushwant Singh | novel |
| *A Suitable Boy* | Vikram Seth | novel |
| *Unbreakable* | M. C. Mary Kom | autobiography |

A book’s award history, bestseller rank, or latest edition is separate from authorship. Verify current prizes and newly released books by edition and official announcement.

**Self-check**

Why is “*Wings of Fire* — A. P. J. Abdul Kalam” incomplete in a precision question?

<details>
<summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

The autobiography is credited to A. P. J. Abdul Kalam **with Arun Tiwari**. Some short exam keys cite Kalam alone, but a question asking for full authorship should include both.
</details>
