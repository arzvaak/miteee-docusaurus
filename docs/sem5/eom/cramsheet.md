## 📚 **CHAPTER 1: BASIS OF MANAGEMENT THEORY & PRACTICE**

### 🔑 **MANAGER DEFINITION**
**Manager**: Individual responsible for **POLC** (Planning, Organizing, Leading, Controlling) to achieve organizational goals

**Organization**: Deliberate arrangement of people to accomplish specific purpose

---

### ⚙️ **5 MANAGEMENT FUNCTIONS** 
**Memory Trick: "Please Organize Staff's Day Carefully"**

1. **PLANNING** = Selecting missions, objectives & actions
   - Requires decision-making
   - Choosing future courses from alternatives

2. **ORGANIZING** = Creating intentional structure
   - Establishing roles for people
   - Assigning tasks to those who can do them best

3. **STAFFING** = Filling positions in organization
   - Recruiting, selecting, placing, promoting
   - Training, appraising, compensating

4. **DIRECTING/LEADING** = Influencing people
   - Motivation, leadership styles
   - Communication

5. **CONTROLLING** = Measuring & correcting performance
   - Ensuring events conform to plans
   - Showing deviations from standards
   - Correcting deviations

---

### 🎨 **3 MANAGERIAL SKILLS (Katz Model)**
**Memory: "Technical Human Concepts"**

| Skill Level | Top Management | Middle Management | Lower Management |
|-------------|----------------|-------------------|------------------|
| **CONCEPTUAL** | HIGH (Big picture) | MEDIUM | LOW |
| **HUMAN** | HIGH | HIGH (Most important!) | HIGH |
| **TECHNICAL** | LOW | MEDIUM | HIGH (Most important!) |

**Key Point**: 
- Bottom = Technical skills dominate
- Middle = Human skills most critical  
- Top = Conceptual skills dominate

## 📚 **CHAPTER 2: ESSENTIALS OF PLANNING**

### 📋 **8 TYPES OF PLANS**
**Memory: "MP-GO-SP-PR-RU-PG-BU"**

1. **Mission/Vision** - Basic purpose
2. **Goals/Objectives** - Specific targets
3. **Strategy** - Overall game plan
4. **Policy** - General guidelines
5. **Procedure** - Step-by-step process
6. **Rules** - No flexibility! (e.g., "No smoking")
7. **Programs** - Complex package
8. **Budget** - Numberized plans

**Classification Trick:**
- **Time:** Long (5+ yrs) vs Short (&lt;1 yr)
- **Breadth:** Strategic (whole org) vs Tactical (part)
- **Specificity:** Specific (clear) vs Directional (flexible)
- **Frequency:** Single-use vs Standing plans

---

### 🎯 **MBO (Management by Objectives)**
**Key Features - "VHNM":**
- **V**erifiable (measurable)
- **H**ierarchy (cascade down)
- **N**etwork (interconnected)
- **M**ultiplicity (multiple objectives)

**Benefits:** ✅ Better planning, participation, motivation
**Weaknesses:** ❌ Time-consuming, short-term focus, inflexibility

**SMART Objectives:**
- **S**pecific
- **M**easurable
- **A**chievable
- **R**elevant
- **T**ime-bound

---

### 📊 **STRATEGIC PLANNING TOOLS**
**5 Tools: "CBBSP" (Critical, Blue, BCG, SWOT, Porter)**

#### **1. Critical Question Analysis**
- "What is our business?"
- "Who are our customers?"

#### **2. SWOT Analysis**
**Internal:** Strengths, Weaknesses
**External:** Opportunities, Threats

**TOWS Matrix (Strategy Formation):**
- **SO** = Use Strengths to grab Opportunities (Maxi-Maxi)
- **ST** = Use Strengths to avoid Threats (Maxi-Mini)
- **WO** = Overcome Weaknesses for Opportunities (Mini-Maxi)
- **WT** = Minimize both (Mini-Mini) - Defensive!

#### **3. Blue Ocean Strategy**
**RED Ocean:** Compete in existing market (bloody!)
**BLUE Ocean:** Create new market space

**4 Actions Framework: "ERRC"**
- **E**liminate factors
- **R**educe factors
- **R**aise factors
- **C**reate new factors

#### **4. BCG Matrix (Portfolio Analysis)**

```
     HIGH Growth
      ↑
  ⭐ STARS  | ❓ QUESTION MARKS
  -----------+-----------
  🐄 CASH COWS | 🐕 DOGS
      ↓
     LOW Growth
```

**Formulas (IMPORTANT!):**
- Market Growth Rate = (This year - Last year) / Last year × 100
- Relative Market Share = Your sales / Competitor's sales

**Strategies:**
- ⭐ **Stars:** INVEST heavily
- ❓ **Question Marks:** DECIDE (invest or divest)
- 🐄 **Cash Cows:** MILK profits
- 🐕 **Dogs:** DIVEST

#### **5. Porter's Generic Strategies**

```
           LOW COST | DIFFERENTIATION
BROAD:    Cost Lead | Differentiation
NARROW:   Cost Focus| Diff Focus
```

- **Cost Leadership:** Compete on price (Walmart)
- **Differentiation:** Unique features (Apple)
- **Focus:** Target niche market

**⚠️ STUCK IN MIDDLE = NO strategy = FAIL!**

## 📚 **CHAPTER 3: ORGANIZING FUNCTION**

### 🏗️ **SPAN OF CONTROL**
**7 Factors Affecting Span: "STPRCKA"**
- **S**ubordinates' training
- **T**ime available
- **P**lanning (clear plans = wider span)
- **R**ate of change
- **C**ommunication techniques
- **K**ind of activity
- **A**mount of delegation

**Rule:** 
- Well-trained staff + Clear plans + Stable environment = WIDE span ✅
- Complex work + Rapid change = NARROW span ✅

### ⚖️ **AUTHORITY, RESPONSIBILITY, DELEGATION**

**Golden Rule:**
```
Authority = Responsibility (PERFECT!)

Authority > Responsibility = Abuse of power ❌
Authority < Responsibility = Frustration ❌
```

**Centralization vs Decentralization:**
- **Centralized:** Decisions at TOP
- **Decentralized:** Decisions at LOWER levels
- **Best:** Find BALANCE! (Never absolute)

**Think:** HRM manages, HRD develops!
