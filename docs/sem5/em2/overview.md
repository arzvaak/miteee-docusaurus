# Electrical Machines II

Welcome to the week-wise study notes for **Electrical Machines II**. These notes are compiled from lecture transcripts, the course textbook, and previous year question banks.

## How to Use These Notes

Each week covers a major topic with:
- **Theory & Concepts** — Key principles, derivations, and explanations
- **Diagrams & Phasors** — Board-work images from lectures
- **Solved Examples** — Numerical problems with step-by-step solutions
- **End-of-Week Questions** — Previous year questions with detailed answers

## Course Topics

The notes are organized in the sidebar by week. Start from Week 1 and work your way through, or jump to a specific topic.

All mathematical expressions use $\KaTeX$ rendering:

## Prerequisites

A solid understanding of **Electrical Machines I** concepts including:
- Single-phase transformers
- DC machines (generators and motors)
- Basic electromagnetic theory
- Three-phase AC fundamentals
